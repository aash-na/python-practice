Here are the descriptions of a few mini python projects i did !
* BASIC CALCULATOR

> A console-based calculator program that performs basic arithmetic operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
> The program displays a simple menu of operations to the user. 
> After choosing an operation, you enter two numbers and the calculator outputs the result. 
> The menu repeats until you decide to exit.

* EXPENSE TRACKER

> A simple console-based budget tracker to manage your finances.
> You can input your incomes and expenses (optionally grouped by category) and the program will keep track of your total balance.
> It shows a summary of total income, total expenses, and remaining balance, plus a breakdown by category.
> This helps you understand where your money is coming from and going to.

* FILE ORGANIZER

> A utility script that organizes files in a specified directory into subfolders based on their file extensions.
> it can move all .jpg and .png files into an "Images" folder, .mp3 files into an "Audio" folder, and so on.
> This helps keep a folder neat and tidy by grouping similar file types together.

* FLASHCARD

> A simple console-based flashcard quiz application.
> You can create flashcards by entering a term and its definition. 
> Then the app can quiz you by showing each term and asking for the definition.
> This helps with memorization and self-testing for studying vocabulary, language learning, or any subject with questions and answers.
> The app keeps track of your score during each quiz session.

* NUMBER GUESSING GAME
> A simple console game where the computer picks a random number and you try to guess it.
> After each guess, the program tells you whether your guess is too high or too low.
> The game continues until you guess the correct number, and then it displays the number of attempts you took.

* PASSWORD GENERATOR
> A simple password generator that generates secure random passwords based on user-defined criteria.
> Generates passwords of a specified length
> Includes uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols
